# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React 19 + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind CSS 4, react-router, lucide-react. Existing codebase; not a user decision to revisit.

## Users

- Students (school and college) in Chennai seeking hands-on robotics/electronics/IoT training and project help; parents researching for them.
- Schools and colleges wanting structured programmes.
- Makers and builders buying components (Arduino, ESP32, sensors).
- Homeowners and small businesses wanting IoT/automation.
- Farmers and agricultural institutions interested in AGRINEX.

## Product Purpose

Marketing/enquiry site for VISRONIX Technologies, a multidisciplinary technology company in Chennai. Six divisions: STEM education & training, electronic components store, IoT & smart home, engineering project support, AGRINEX agricultural robotics (flagship in-house product), research & innovation. Success = a visitor sends a WhatsApp enquiry.

## Positioning

One company where students train on the same components the company stocks and builds with; prototypes become products, products feed back into teaching. AGRINEX is an in-house agricultural robot engineered for real farm conditions — not a showroom demo.

## Operating Context

Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. WhatsApp is the primary contact channel (+91 99620 11415). Visitors often arrive on mobile via WhatsApp/Instagram referrals. No backend; forms hand off to WhatsApp/email.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static SPA, zero-budget hosting (Cloudflare Pages/Netlify).
- No backend; enquiry forms prepare prefilled messages, never auto-send.
- Email address not yet provided by client (env-gated, honest fallback states).
- Product catalogue is demonstration data until client supplies real stock/prices.
- Portfolio limited to AGRINEX (prototype stage) until verified work completes.
- Team profiles, achievements, social links pending client confirmation.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: VISRONIX Technologies; logo mark "V" circuit motif.
- Palette pinned by client brief: ink #08111F, cyan #37D6F5 accent (dark, premium, technical).
- Quality bar: Apple-inspired design language (the client brief's words).
- Voice: plain, honest, confident; states what is real and what is pending.

## Evidence on Hand

- WhatsApp number: +91 99620 11415 (verified, from client).
- No email, no prices, no testimonials, no team data, no social links, no photography. All must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Honesty is the brand: show real status, label placeholders, never fake proof.
2. One company, six divisions — cross-link them; a student is a future IoT client.
3. WhatsApp-first: every page ends one tap from a prefilled enquiry.
4. Show the build: demonstration over claim wherever possible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast; keyboard navigable; reduced-motion respected; mobile-first (most visitors on phones).
